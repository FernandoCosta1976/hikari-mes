# PERSONA-002 — Production Leader

**Canonical role:** Production Leader  
**UI reference:** Líder de Produção  
**Layer:** Operational / Tactical  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Mission

Coordinate execution during the shift, ensure that released work is progressing, support Resource/Lot organization and intervene when the production commitment is at risk.

## Primary questions

- O que precisamos produzir neste turno?
- O que está produzindo agora?
- O que vem depois?
- Estamos cumprindo a quantidade comprometida?
- Existe algum Lot atrasado ou em risco?
- Qual Resource está disponível?
- Existe Setup desnecessário?
- Temos matéria-prima?
- Como está o buffer?
- Onde preciso intervir agora?

## Interaction moments

1. review shift commitment;
2. review Hour-by-Hour Plan;
3. work with Supervisor on operational sequence;
4. support Resource assignment;
5. monitor execution;
6. react to events;
7. assess shift completion.

## Required information

- scheduled Lots;
- shift/day quantities;
- Scheduled Start/Finish;
- operational sequence;
- Resource assignment;
- Resource constraints;
- Setup context;
- raw-material risk;
- current/projected buffer;
- actual production;
- events and deviations.

## Decisions/actions

- jointly organize Lots with Supervisor;
- jointly assign Resources;
- coordinate floor execution;
- prioritize intervention;
- escalate constraints.

## Boundary

The Production Leader does not redefine the corporate planning requirement. Operational flexibility is exercised within the production commitment.

## Experience principle

The Leader needs a short horizon and actionable information. HIKARI should highlight what requires attention instead of forcing manual reconciliation across multiple screens.
