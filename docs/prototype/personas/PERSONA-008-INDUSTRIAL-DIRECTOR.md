# PERSONA-008 — Industrial Director

**Canonical role:** Industrial Director  
**UI reference:** Diretor Industrial  
**Layer:** Executive  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Mission

Understand operational efficiency and production predictability across the industrial system and decide where leadership attention and investment are required.

## Dominant questions

Two questions govern the executive HIKARI experience:

### 1. Qual é nossa eficiência operacional?

Supporting questions:

- Onde estamos perdendo eficiência?
- Quanto estamos perdendo?
- Qual área/processo está degradando performance?
- Como disponibilidade, performance e qualidade se comportam?
- Onde está o gargalo?

### 2. Temos previsibilidade do que precisamos produzir e de nossa capacidade de cumprir?

Supporting questions:

- O plano está sendo cumprido?
- Vamos cumprir os próximos compromissos?
- Onde existe risco de falta?
- Como está a proteção dos buffers?
- Quais áreas exigem atenção?
- Qual desvio pode comprometer a cadeia?

## Interaction moments

The Director is not expected to operate the Hour-by-Hour Plan.

The Director consumes information generated by the operational/tactical journey through:

- executive cockpit;
- predictability view;
- operational efficiency;
- OEE/performance;
- strategic risks;
- evolution/coverage.

## Required information

- operational efficiency;
- OEE and components where relevant;
- planned versus actual;
- production projection;
- buffer/flow risk;
- major bottlenecks;
- major losses;
- high-level quality impact;
- first-wave status.

## Decisions/actions

- set direction;
- prioritize leadership attention;
- validate first-wave approach;
- decide whether to advance HIKARI engineering/evolution;
- challenge operational assumptions.

## Must not be shown by default

- APIs;
- schemas;
- payloads;
- event contracts;
- technical broker;
- physical database;
- detailed APF;
- detailed effort by function;
- proprietary algorithms;
- low-level machine configuration.

## Experience principle

Graph first. Executive message second. Detail only on demand.

The prototype succeeds when the Director can say:

**“Agora consigo visualizar como a plataforma funcionará na fábrica e como ela me dará eficiência e previsibilidade.”**
