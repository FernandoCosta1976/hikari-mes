# PERSONA-005 — Manufacturing / Process Engineer

**Role:** Manufacturing Engineer / Process Engineer  
**UI reference:** Engenheiro de Manufatura / Processo  
**Layer:** Tactical / Specialist  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Mission

Analyze recurring constraints, process behavior, performance degradation and structural improvement opportunities.

## Primary questions

- Onde estão ocorrendo perdas recorrentes?
- Qual Resource/processo concentra restrições?
- Qual Setup está degradando o fluxo?
- Onde está o gargalo?
- O comportamento é pontual ou recorrente?
- Quais Materials/Lots são mais impactados?
- Existe oportunidade de melhoria de capacidade/processo?

## Interaction moments

Primarily after operational data exists:

- deviation analysis;
- bottleneck analysis;
- loss analysis;
- performance analysis;
- continuous improvement.

## Required information

- historical/aggregated execution;
- Resource;
- Material;
- Lot;
- Setup;
- events;
- losses;
- throughput;
- quality context;
- adherence/performance indicators.

## Decisions/actions

- investigate cause;
- propose process improvement;
- prioritize engineering analysis;
- support capacity and process decisions.

## Boundary

The executive prototype shall not expose proprietary algorithms, detailed engineering parameters or full process recipes.

## Experience principle

Engineering requires drill-down and correlation, but the prototype should progressively disclose detail rather than placing engineering density on executive or operator screens.
