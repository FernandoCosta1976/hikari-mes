# PERSONA-007 — Production Manager

**Canonical role:** Production Manager  
**UI reference:** Gerente de Produção  
**Layer:** Tactical / Managerial  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Mission

Ensure the area is meeting production commitments, identify risks and bottlenecks, understand losses and direct intervention across shifts/resources.

## Primary questions

- Estamos cumprindo o plano?
- Vamos cumprir o dia?
- Qual área/Resource está em risco?
- Onde está o gargalo?
- Quanto perdemos?
- Como está o buffer?
- Quais Materials estão abaixo da proteção desejada?
- Qual é a aderência planejado × executado?
- Quais eventos estão degradando performance?
- Qual decisão precisa de escalonamento?

## Interaction moments

- start-of-day/shift review;
- periodic operational review;
- deviation escalation;
- performance review;
- projected completion review.

## Required information

- plan versus actual;
- projected completion;
- buffer current/projected;
- bottlenecks;
- events;
- losses;
- quality;
- Resource performance;
- shift/day commitment.

## Decisions/actions

- prioritize managerial intervention;
- coordinate cross-functional response;
- protect daily commitment;
- escalate systemic risks.

## Experience principle

The Manager requires exception-driven aggregation with the ability to drill into the operational cause.
