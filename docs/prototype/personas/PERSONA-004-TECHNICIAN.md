# PERSONA-004 — Technician

**Role:** Technician  
**UI reference:** Técnico  
**Layer:** Operational / Specialist  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Mission

Understand and act on technical conditions that constrain production execution.

## Primary questions

- Qual Resource está com restrição?
- O que impede a continuidade?
- Qual Lot/Material é afetado?
- Há uma condição de máquina, molde ou ferramental?
- Qual é o impacto operacional?
- A intervenção foi concluída?

## Interaction moments

The Technician is engaged when readiness or execution identifies a technical restriction or event.

## Required information

- Resource;
- affected Lot;
- Material;
- event/restriction;
- timing;
- impact on production;
- contextual maintenance/tooling information when available.

## Decisions/actions

- diagnose within role responsibility;
- register/interact with occurrence;
- indicate restoration/condition;
- provide technical context to Leader/Supervisor.

## Boundary

The first prototype does not define a maintenance-management system, work-order process or predictive-maintenance architecture.

## Experience principle

Technical detail must be contextual and action-oriented. The Technician should not have to navigate executive dashboards to find the affected Resource.
