# WF-001 — Plan Commitment and Summary

## 1. Purpose

Provide an immediate quantitative answer to:

**Quanto precisamos produzir?**

without turning WF-001 into an executive dashboard.

## 2. Recommended commitment strip

Potential elements:

### Planejado hoje
Total quantity represented by the displayed plan.

### Lots
Number of scheduled Lots.

### Compromisso do turno
When a valid shift context exists.

### Destino
Compact distribution such as:
- Montagem;
- Reposição;
- Engenharia.

### Atenções
Count of items requiring review.

## 3. Avoid KPI inflation

Do not add:

- OEE;
- Availability;
- Performance;
- Quality;
- Scrap;
- downtime;
- bottleneck;

to this strip.

Those answer different questions.

## 4. Quantity reconciliation

The displayed planned total must be derived consistently from the demonstrative Lots for the selected horizon.

If PyMAC quantity differs, do not silently choose one total.

Expose reconciliation attention.

## 5. Destination aggregation

Destination totals must sum coherently to the scheduled Lot total.

Example:

Planejado: 1,200

- Montagem: 1,000
- Reposição: 100
- Engenharia: 100

This is illustrative.

## 6. Shift/day distinction

If viewing a shift, clearly distinguish:

**Compromisso do turno**

from:

**Compromisso do dia**

Avoid presenting one as the other.

## 7. Future projection

Projected completion belongs primarily to execution/managerial experiences, not the initial scheduling summary.

Do not introduce forecast indicators before execution context exists.
