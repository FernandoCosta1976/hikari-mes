# WF-001 — Responsive and Density Rules

## 1. Primary target

The executive/industrial prototype is primarily designed for desktop/laptop presentation.

Recommended design baseline:
large desktop viewport suitable for meeting-room demonstration.

## 2. Responsiveness

Responsive behavior is required, but mobile operation is not the primary optimization target for WF-001.

## 3. Large desktop

Use:

- full timeline;
- contextual side rail;
- right drawer;
- commitment strip.

## 4. Medium desktop/laptop

Collapse secondary attention rail into compact strip.

Preserve timeline width as much as possible.

## 5. Tablet

Timeline may become horizontally scrollable.

Lot detail may overlay rather than sit beside the timeline.

## 6. Mobile

If supported, mobile is a consultation mode.

Do not force the full planning board into a compressed unreadable grid.

A vertical list/time sequence may replace the full desktop timeline.

## 7. Density

Industrial applications require information density.

Density must come from:

- alignment;
- typography hierarchy;
- concise labels;
- progressive disclosure;

not from tiny text.

## 8. Minimum legibility

Do not reduce labels until Lot identity/timing become difficult to read in a presentation.

## 9. Meeting-room visibility

Critical numbers and selected states must remain legible when screen-shared/projected.

## 10. Horizontal scrolling

Acceptable for the timeline when it preserves temporal accuracy.

Avoid horizontal scrolling for general page chrome and summaries.
