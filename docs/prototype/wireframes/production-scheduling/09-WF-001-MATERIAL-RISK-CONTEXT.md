# WF-001 — Material Risk Context

## 1. Purpose

Signal whether raw-material availability may compromise the scheduled commitment.

## 2. Compact representation

Example:

**Matéria-prima · suficiente**

or:

**Matéria-prima · atenção**

## 3. Contextual detail

When attention exists:

**Material/Lot afetado**  
**Risco de insuficiência para o compromisso planejado**

Where demonstrative quantities are available, show them coherently.

## 4. Scope

The user needs operational relevance, not MRP detail.

Do not expose:

- full MRP run;
- detailed procurement;
- supplier planning;
- complete BOM explosion.

## 5. Timeline relation

Affected Lots may receive an attention marker.

The timeline remains readable and does not become a material-planning grid.

## 6. Readiness transition

If material risk requires deeper evaluation, provide a path to:

**Preparação para Produção**

This establishes continuity between WF-001 and the next experience.

## 7. Unknown data

If material availability is unavailable:

**Disponibilidade de matéria-prima não informada**

Do not convert missing data into “sufficient”.
