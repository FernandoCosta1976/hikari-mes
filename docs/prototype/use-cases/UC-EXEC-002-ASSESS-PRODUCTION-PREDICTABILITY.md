# UC-EXEC-002 — Assess Production Predictability

**Primary actor:** Industrial Director  
**Supporting actor:** Production Manager  
**Question:** Temos previsibilidade do que precisamos produzir e de nossa capacidade de cumprir?  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Required information

- production requirement;
- current execution;
- projected completion;
- buffer current/projected;
- major constraints;
- downstream flow context;
- material risk;
- plan freshness.

## Main flow

1. Director sees current production commitment.
2. HIKARI shows expected fulfillment/projection.
3. HIKARI highlights material/buffer/flow risks.
4. Director understands which areas require attention.

## Outcome

Leadership has an anticipatory view rather than only retrospective production reporting.

## Strategic rule

Predictability is one of the two dominant executive outcomes of HIKARI, together with operational efficiency.
