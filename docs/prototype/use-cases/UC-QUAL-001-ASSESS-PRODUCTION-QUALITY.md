# UC-QUAL-001 — Assess Production Quality

**Primary actor:** Quality Professional  
**Supporting actors:** Production Manager, Engineer, Supervisor  
**Question:** Minha produção perdeu a qualidade?  
**Status:** PROTOTYPE_BASELINE

## Required information

- produced quantity;
- good quantity;
- Scrap;
- Rework;
- blocked quantity when applicable;
- Lot;
- Material;
- Resource;
- quality component of OEE.

## Outcome

Quality impact on usable output and operational performance is understood.

## Rule

Produced quantity shall not be equated to downstream-available quantity when release conditions prevent consumption.
